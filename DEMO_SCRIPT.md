# 🎙️ SkillSwap — 3-Minute Hackathon Presentation Script

> **Instructions for Speaker**: Practice reading this aloud at a natural, confident pace. Keep the browser window open at `http://localhost:3000/login` before starting!

---

### ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:15 | Step 1: The Problem
> *"Judges, every semester millions of students have skills they’re willing to teach — like React, Data Science, or French — but they also need help with other subjects like UI Design or Physics. Traditional tutoring is expensive, and finding someone who wants an exact mutual skill swap by hand is almost impossible. That’s why we built **SkillSwap** — an intelligent peer-to-peer student skill exchange network."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:15 – 0:35 | Step 2: Quick Demo Login
> *"Let’s log in right now. I’ll click our one-click quick demo login to sign in as **Sarah Chen**, a senior full-stack developer who teaches React and TypeScript, but wants to learn UI Design and Figma. As soon as Sarah logs in, her dashboard gives her an instant view of her active skill portfolio, rating score, and top suggested matches."*

---

### ⏱️ 0:35 – 1:20 | Step 3: The Centerpiece — Match Matrix
*(Click on **"Matches"** in the navigation bar)*

> *"Now, this page is the centerpiece of SkillSwap: our **Reciprocal Match Matrix**. Notice how **Alex Rivera** is ranked as Sarah’s #1 match at **99% Compatibility**."*

*(Point to the Reciprocal Swap Badge)*
> *"First, our algorithm identified a **2-Way Reciprocal Swap** — Sarah teaches React which Alex wants, and Alex teaches UI Design which Sarah wants."*

*(Point to the Matched Skill Chips)*
> *"Look at these visual skill chips: green shows what Sarah teaches, and purple shows what Alex teaches."*

*(Click **"Show Details"** on the Visual Score Breakdown Accordion)*
> *"If we expand the visual score breakdown, you can see the exact math powering our algorithm: Alex got **50 points** for the 2-way reciprocal match, **25 points** for his Expert skill level fit, **15 points** for matching weekend availability, and **9.6 points** for his high 4.8 star community rating! This transparency lets students trust why a match is recommended."*

---

### ⏱️ 1:20 – 1:50 | Step 4: Propose & Accept Exchange
*(Click **"Propose Skill Swap with Alex Rivera"**, click **"Send Exchange Request"**)*

> *"Now Sarah clicks 'Propose Skill Swap' and sends a request. Now let's switch roles instantly. I'll log out and click quick login as **Alex Rivera**."*

*(Log out -> Click Quick Login as Alex Rivera -> Click **"Requests"** in Navbar)*
> *"When Alex opens his Requests tab, he immediately sees Sarah's incoming proposal to swap Figma for React. Alex clicks **Accept**, establishing our active skill exchange!"*

---

### ⏱️ 1:50 – 2:10 | Step 5: Mark Complete & Rate Exchange Partner
*(Click **"Mark Complete & Rate"** -> Rating Modal opens)*

> *"Once their learning session is done, Alex clicks **'Mark Complete & Rate'**. This opens our trust modal. Alex selects **5 Stars**, writes a review: *'Awesome React session!'*, and clicks Submit."*

*(Show success checkmark)*
> *"Behind the scenes, the system automatically recalculates Sarah's weighted average rating and updates her profile reputation score in real time. If Alex tries to rate this session a second time, our system safely blocks duplicate ratings."*

---

### ⏱️ 2:10 – 2:20 | Step 6: Conclusion & Tech Stack
> *"SkillSwap was built with Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and React with Tailwind CSS. Next, we plan to add real-time Socket.io chat and video session scheduling. Thank you, and we’d love to take your questions!"*
